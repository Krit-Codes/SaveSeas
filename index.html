5 Why's

  1. Why is coral dying?Dredging stirs up mud that clouds the water, blocks sunlight and smothers coral.
  2. Why is there dredging?Builders dig sand from the seabed to make islands and new land.
  3. Why do they use sand from the sea?It is the closest and cheapest building material.
  4. Why does cheap matter so much?Saving time and money matters more to builders than protecting reefs.
  5. Root causeWhy don't they care about reefs?Companies don't pay for the damage they do to nature.

Why coral reefs matter

Coastal construction killing UAE coral reefs

0.2%
Coral reefs cover only 0.2% of the ocean floor but support at least 25% of all marine species.
100s of millions
Hundreds of millions of people depend on reefs for food, jobs and storm protection.
97%
Reefs reduce wave energy by an average of 97%, protecting about 197 million people from storms and floods.
